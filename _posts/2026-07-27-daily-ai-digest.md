### Amnezia VPN Client - Self-Hosted VPN Client with Traffic Obfuscation
*   What it does
    An open-source VPN client designed for deploying and managing your own VPN server. It automates the installation of VPN services (like OpenVPN, WireGuard) via Docker containers on your server using simple SSH credentials.
*   Key features
    *   **Easy Self-Hosting:** Automatically installs and configures VPN server containers with a few clicks.
    *   **Multiple Protocols:** Supports classic (OpenVPN, WireGuard, IKEv2) and obfuscation protocols (OpenVPN over Cloak, Shadowsocks, AmneziaWG, XRay) to bypass network restrictions.

    *   **Split Tunneling:** Allows routing only specific websites or apps through the VPN.
*   Why it's notable
    It stands out by making personal, self-hosted VPN deployment accessible to non-experts while providing advanced obfuscation features crucial for users in regions with heavy internet censorship or privacy concerns. Its active development and recent star growth highlight its popularity in the self-hosting and privacy community.
